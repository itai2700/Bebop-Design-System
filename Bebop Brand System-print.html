Option 03 · Lime + Magenta pop

The mark

One wordmark, one weight. Bebop. set in Archivo Black at -3pt letter-spacing, with a single accent dot at descender level — the only ornament the brand needs.

The colorways

Two official lockups. A · Primary places Ink Black on Paper with a Lime dot. B · Reversed flips to Paper on Ink with a Magenta dot. The dot is always a circle.

The law

Lime is the identity. Magenta is the pop, reserved for dark surfaces and special moments. Never the other way around. The mark must also hold up in pure black & white at favicon size.
